FAQ

Key distinctions about direct and trackable QRs.

Are Contact and Link/Text QRs tracked?

No. They encode their information directly and do not route scans through the tracking service.

What makes the Trackable option different?

It contains a redirect URL. A scan can be logged before the visitor is sent to the destination, so it requires an online service.

Does scan count equal unique people or devices?

No. The same person or device can scan more than once. Without cookies or persistent fingerprinting, repeat-device identification is not reliable. IP address and browser information can provide clues, but they are not dependable unique-device identifiers.

Can a Contact QR work without internet?

Yes, if the phone's scanner recognizes the vCard data directly. The contact information is inside the QR.

What are the advanced settings?

Error correction, image resolution, and intended physical size are available under Advanced settings. Their defaults are chosen for a practical general-purpose balance.
